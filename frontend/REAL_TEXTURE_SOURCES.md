# Real planet texture source

Primary texture source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

The source page states that the pack contains real planetary maps in equirectangular projection for Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Earth, Sun, Moon, and stars. It says the textures are based on NASA elevation and imagery data, with colors tuned against spacecraft and Hubble true-color imagery. The page states the textures are distributed under Attribution 4.0 International and may be used, adapted, and shared for any purpose, including commercial use.

NASA reference: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/

NASA describes its 3D Resources as free to download and use, mirrored on GitHub, subject to NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines.
